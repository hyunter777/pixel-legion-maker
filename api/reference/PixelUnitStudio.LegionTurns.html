Instead of rotating weapon art, swaps in whichever pre-rotated clean art (turns, counter-clockwise k×step°) is closest to the weapon angle. Rotating pixel art at an arbitrary angle at runtime breaks outlines and drops or overlaps pixels (tools/generate_weapon_turns.py). Attached to a renderer that is a child of the weapon bone. The motion decides the bone rotation as is, and this renderer cancels that rotation and changes only the art's direction.
